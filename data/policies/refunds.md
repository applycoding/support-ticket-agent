# Refund Policy

## Window

Change-of-mind refund window: a refund is available for 30 days after delivery.
Unopened items use the same refund window. A defect refund is available for 90 days after delivery.
A broken, damaged, or defective item uses that defect refund.
The refund covers the remaining balance on the order.

## Limits

The automatic approval limit is $75.00. A refund at or under $75.00 can be approved by the agent.
A refund above $75.00 is parked for a teammate. No money moves until that approval.
Do not refund more than the remaining balance. Do not refund an order that was already refunded.

## Cancellation

A cancellation refund is allowed only when the order has not shipped.

# Shipping Policy

## Transit

A package that is in transit for fewer than 7 days is still inside the normal shipping time.
Share the shipment date and the current status. Do not offer a refund yet.

## Courtesy

A package in transit for 7 to 13 days can receive a courtesy credit of $15.00.
The courtesy credit cannot be larger than the remaining balance.

## Lost package

A lost package is a package still in transit for 14 days or more.
The remedy is a refund of the remaining balance, subject to the approval limit.
